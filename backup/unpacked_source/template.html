Our approach

Treatment that understands the whole you

We don't just chase symptoms. Every journey starts by understanding how you live and move, then builds a plan you can actually keep up with.

01

Full physical assessment

A 45-minute evaluation of posture, mobility, strength and history to find the real source — not just the sore spot.

02

Hands-on treatment

Targeted manual therapy and guided movement in clinic to restore range and settle pain from session one.

03

Home exercise plan

A personalised video programme you follow between visits, so progress sticks long after you leave the clinic.
