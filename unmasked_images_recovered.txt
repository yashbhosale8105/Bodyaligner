I have fixed the blurriness of the **MOVEMENT** word-image:

### Why was it blurry?
The native height of the `Video_background_for_physiothera__202606071803-removebg-preview.png` image is `373px`. In the previous change, setting the CSS height of that image to `392px` (coupled with Windows high-DPI system display scaling) forced the browser to stretch the pixels beyond their native resolution, causing the text edges to look blurry/pixelated.

### How I fixed it:
1. **Scaled Down to Crisp Resolution**: 
   - Reduced `--keyword-height` on desktop back to a max of `12rem` (192px).
   - Reduced the `--movement-scale` multiplier from `1.75` to `1.55`. 
   - This keeps the "MOVEMENT" image capped at a maximum of `297px` tall in CSS. At this size, the image stays well below its native height of `373px` (even on high-DPI screens), forcing the browser to scale it down and rendering it **perfectly crisp and sharp**.
2. **Balanced Spacing & Base Font Size**:
   - Adjusted `.title-base` ("RELIEF FROM / RESTORE") to `clamp(2.4rem, 4.6vw, 3.8rem)` to keep it scaled nicely with the sharp images.
   - Updated the negative margins to `margin-block: -2.3rem` (for "PAIN") and `margin-block: -5.6rem` (for "MOVEMENT") to keep the layout boxes matching perfectly at exactly `118.4px` tall, keeping the lines tightly stacked with zero vertical gap.
